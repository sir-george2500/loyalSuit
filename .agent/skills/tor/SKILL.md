Dear Sir/Madam:

The Central Bank of Liberia (CBL) hereby intends to apply funds from its Fiscal Year 2026 budget toward THE DESIGN, SUPPLY, IMPLEMENTATION, AND SUPPORT OF AN E-PROCUREMENT SYSTEM FOR THE BANK UNDER (RFP NO.: CBL/RFP/09/2026).

The Central Bank of Liberia (CBL) invites sealed proposals from qualified and experienced firms for the design, supply, implementation, and support of a robust E-Procurement System. This initiative forms part of the CBL Strategic Plan and represents a transformational effort to strengthen transparency, accountability, efficiency, auditability, and value for money in procurement processes.
The implementation of an E-Procurement System is a key strategic priority aimed at modernizing procurement operations and aligning with international best practices. The initiative seeks to support digital transformation across procurement functions, strengthen internal controls and audit compliance, enhance operational efficiency, reduce procurement cycle times, and improve reporting and data analytics. CBL emphasizes that this initiative is not merely the acquisition of software, but the deployment of a governance tool to enhance institutional integrity and accountability.
The primary objective of this RFP is to procure a secure, scalable, and fully integrated E-Procurement System that will automate end-to-end procurement processes, ensure transparency and traceability of procurement activities, provide comprehensive audit trails, enable data-driven decision-making, and integrate seamlessly with existing financial and ERP systems.
Accordingly, interested eligible bidders may pick up the solicitation document and obtain further information from the General Support Services Department (GSSD), 7th floor, and CBL website, Central Bank of Liberia, Lynch & Ashmun Streets, 1000 Monrovia 10 Liberia, beginning April 8, 2026.

Sealed Proposal must be delivered to the Procurement Section on the 7th floor of the CBL Head Office no later than 2:00 PM on Thursday, April 23, 2026, in hard copies or electronically to procurement@cbl.org.lr . Electronic submission should be protected by passwords. Late proposal will be rejected and returned unopened. This request for proposal (RFP) can also be accessed on the CBL’s website (www.cbl.org.lr).

SCOPE OF WORK AND SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS

The successful bidder shall provide a comprehensive, end-to-end E-Procurement Solution covering system design, configuration, customization, deployment, integration, training, and post-implementation support. The scope shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

1. System Design, Configuration and Customization
   • Conduct a detailed business requirements analysis and gap assessment aligned with CBL procurement policies and regulatory framework.
   • Design a configurable and scalable system architecture tailored to CBL’s operational needs.
   • Customize workflows, approval hierarchies, templates, and user roles.
   • Configure multi-level approvals, thresholds, delegation of authority, and segregation of duties.
   • Provide multilingual capability (if required) and localization features.
2. Procurement Planning and Budgeting Module
   • Annual procurement planning and budget alignment tools.
   • Departmental procurement plan submission, consolidation, and approval workflows.
   • Budget tracking against procurement plans.
   • Automated alerts for budget thresholds and approvals.
3. Supplier/Vendor Management Module
   • Online vendor registration, prequalification, and categorization.
   • Vendor due diligence, compliance checks, and document management.
   • Supplier performance evaluation and rating system.
   • Blacklisting/sanctions management.
4. e-Tendering and Sourcing Module
   • Preparation, approval, and publication of procurement notices and bidding documents.
   • Secure electronic bid submission with encryption and time stamping.
   • Bid opening (electronic) with controlled access and audit logs.
   • Clarifications, addenda, and bidder communications.
   • Support for multiple procurement methods (RFP, RFQ, REOI, etc.).
5. e-Evaluation and Award Module
   • Configurable evaluation criteria and weighted scoring models.
   • Technical and financial bid evaluation workflows.
   • Automated scoring and comparative analysis tools.
   • Evaluation committee collaboration features.
   • Recommendation and approval of awards with full audit trail.
6. e-Auction and Catalogue Management (Optional but Preferred)
   • Support for reverse and forward auctions.
   • Electronic catalogues for framework agreements and pre-approved suppliers.
   • Price comparison and benchmarking tools.
7. Contract Management Module
   • Contract drafting, review, approval, and storage.
   • Contract lifecycle management (CLM).
   • Milestone tracking, deliverables monitoring, and contract amendments.
   • Alerts for contract expiry, renewals, and performance obligations.
8. Purchase Order
   • Automated generation and approval of purchase orders.
   • Integration with contract and supplier data.
   • Goods receipt and service confirmation tracking.
   • Invoice matching (3-way matching capability where applicable).
9. Workflow and Approval Management
   • Fully configurable workflow engine.
   • Rule-based approvals, escalations, and notifications.
   • Real-time tracking of procurement status.
10. Reporting, Analytics and Dashboard
    • Real-time dashboards for procurement performance monitoring.
    • Customizable reports (spend analysis, supplier performance, cycle times, compliance reports).
    • Advanced analytics and data visualization tools.
    • Export capabilities (Excel, PDF, etc.).
11. Audit Trail and Compliance
    • End-to-end audit trail capturing all user actions and system events.
    • Compliance with internal audit requirements and international standards.
    • Tamper-proof logs and reporting.
12. Technical Architecture and Security
    • Secure architecture (cloud or on-premises) with high availability.
    • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and user authentication (including multi-factor authentication).
    • Data encryption (in transit and at rest) using industry standards (e.g., SSL/TLS, AES-256).
    • Compliance with international cybersecurity standards (ISO 27001 or equivalent).
    • Vulnerability management and regular security updates.
13. Integration Requirements
    • Seamless integration with existing financial and ERP systems.
    • API-based integration framework for scalability.
    • Integration with email systems, document management systems, and payment platforms (if applicable).
14. Data Hosting, Backup and Disaster Recovery
    • Flexible deployment options (cloud or on-premises).
    • Compliance with data residency and sovereignty requirements.
    • Automated backup procedures and redundancy.
    • Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) and Business Continuity Plan (BCP) with defined Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
15. Implementation and Deployment
    • Development of a detailed implementation plan with timelines and milestones.
    • Pilot testing and phased rollout strategy.
    • Data migration from legacy/manual systems.
    • System testing (UAT, SIT, security testing).
    • Risk management and mitigation strategies.
16. Change Management and Capacity Building
    • Comprehensive change management strategy.
    • Training programs for different user groups (administrators, procurement staff, vendors, compliance officers).
    • Preparation of user manuals, technical documentation, and SOPs.
    • Knowledge transfer to internal IT and procurement teams.
17. Post-Implementation Support and Maintenance
    • Provision of ongoing technical support (helpdesk, remote and onsite support).
    • Defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs) including response and resolution times.
    • System updates, upgrades, and patches.
    • Performance monitoring and continuous improvement support.
18. Governance and Risk Control
    • Support for procurement governance and compliance monitoring.
    • Built-in controls to prevent fraud, collusion, and process manipulation.
    • Risk registers and mitigation tracking tools.
